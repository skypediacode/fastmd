## From motion to energy

For a body of mass $m$ moving at speed $v$:

$$E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$$

The Gaussian integral is a useful reference:

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}$$
