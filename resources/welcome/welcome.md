# Welcome to FastMD

This is a sample Markdown document — feel free to edit it and experiment. Nothing is saved to disk until you choose **File ▸ Save** (`Ctrl+S`).

## Getting around FastMD

FastMD uses familiar shortcuts like **`Ctrl+N`** (New), **`Ctrl+O`** (Open), **`Ctrl+S`** (Save), and **`Ctrl+F`** (Find).

Prefer using the mouse? Most actions are also available from the **toolbar** and **main menu**.

Press **`F1`** to open the **Command Palette** and quickly find other commands.
