Why Choose FastMD?

Built for writers, students, researchers, and developers who need blistering speed, powerful scientific notation, mouse-free productivity, and distraction-free writing.

Instant Startup & Zero Lag

Launches in under 0.5 seconds with a tiny memory footprint (~250 MB). Native C++20 engine guarantees zero typing latency or stutter, even on 50,000+ line documents.

1-Click Math & Chemistry Toolbars

No need to memorize complex LaTeX syntax! Insert KaTeX math equations (matrices, integrals, Greek symbols) and mhchem chemical formulas (\ce{H2O}, reactions) with 1-click visual toolbar previews.

Command Palette & Synonym Search (F1)

Press F1 to search any command by name or smart synonym (e.g. typing pdf, wrap, h1, or table). Execute any menu action instantly without touching the mouse.

Line-Level Bi-Directional Sync

Never lose your place. Double-click any rendered line, heading, or callout in the live preview to jump directly to its source line in the editor. Preview checkboxes update source text interactively.

Mode-Aware Printing & Page Setup

High-fidelity printing (Ctrl+P) that adapts to active mode (Markdown vs Code), customizable paper size/margins (Page Setup), page break controls (Ctrl+Enter), and PDF exports with clickable heading bookmarks.

Mind Maps & 100% Offline Security

Press F10 for interactive mind maps with node-to-source jump. 100% offline with zero cloud connections, zero telemetry, no user account required, no popups, and no subscription fees.
